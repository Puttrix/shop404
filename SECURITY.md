# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

- `main`: actively supported and patched as needed

## Reporting a Vulnerability

- Email the maintainers at [security@shop404.dev](mailto:security@shop404.dev) with a description, impact, and reproduction steps.
- Please do not open a public issue for security reports.
- We aim to acknowledge new reports within 3 business days and provide a remediation plan or status update within 10 business days.

## Preferred Languages

- English

## Additional Notes

- If you need to share sensitive artifacts, request a secure upload link in your initial email.
- Thank you for helping keep Shop404 users safe.
